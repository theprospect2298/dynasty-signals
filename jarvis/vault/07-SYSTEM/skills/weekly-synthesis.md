# weekly-synthesis

## Purpose
Synthesize the week's accumulated knowledge and activity into a review that
surfaces what the individual days couldn't show.

## Trigger
Scheduled every Sunday at 7:00 PM.
Manual: "Weekly synthesis" or "Week review"

## Process

STEP 1: Gather the week
Read all daily notes from the past 7 days.
Read all permanent notes created this week.
Read all connection notes from this week.
Read all morning briefs from this week.
Read CLAUDE.md for current priorities and beliefs.

STEP 2: Assess the week honestly
What actually happened versus what was planned?
Which projects moved and which stalled?
What was the quality of decisions made?
What type of work dominated the week?

STEP 3: Identify what only the week view reveals
What theme appeared across multiple days?
What pattern exists that is invisible in any single day?
Did any belief in CLAUDE.md get confirmed or challenged?
Did any connection note change how you think about a current project?

STEP 4: Prepare next week
What is the single most important thing next week?
Based on this week's evidence not on intentions.
What needs to be decided before next week starts?
What CLAUDE.md updates does this week support?

STEP 5: Generate synthesis

## Quality Standard
The synthesis must say something no individual note from the week says.
If it just summarizes what you already know it has failed. It should produce at
least one insight that requires the full week's data to generate.
